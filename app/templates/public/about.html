How {{ settings.app_name }} verifies its data

This directory lists blood banks in Haldwani only when their details can be traced to a source. It does not guess addresses, phone numbers, map locations or stock.

Sources we use

  1. The blood bank itself: a recorded call, an email or a letter from the facility.
  2. Official government sources, such as the e-RaktKosh portal (MoHFW) and state and national blood centre lists.
  3. The hospital's own official website.
  4. Site visits, for locations only.

Third-party directories and community maps are used only as leads. They can never confirm a detail on their own.

What the labels mean

Checked against an official source
The detail matches a government source or the hospital's official website on the date shown.
Confirmed by the facility
The blood bank confirmed it directly.
Verified on site
The location was checked in person.
Not yet confirmed
The detail comes from a listed source but hasn't been checked directly. Treat it with care.

Checks are dated and expire. A blood bank whose identity check is out of date, disputed or missing is removed from the public directory until it is re-verified.

The map

A blood bank gets a pin only when its coordinates come from a recorded source and its location has been verified. Other listed blood banks appear in the list with their address and no pin. Map tiles and data are © OpenStreetMap contributors. Directions links hand you over to Google Maps or OpenStreetMap.

Blood availability

Availability appears only when a participating blood bank publishes it itself. An authorised administrator or medical officer chooses to publish a summary of the blood bags that are ready to issue (screened, not reserved and not expired). The figures are counted per blood group for whole blood.

  • Each blood bank chooses to show either a level (Available / Low / Not available) or an exact count.
  • Published figures expire after at most 24 hours (sooner if the blood bank chooses). Expired figures are never shown.
  • If the blood bank's stock falls after publishing, the lower current figure is shown. It is never shown higher.
  • Availability is not a reservation and does not guarantee that blood can be issued. Always call before travelling.

No donor, patient or individual blood bag information is ever published. Blood banks that don't publish are shown as "not published". You can also check e-RaktKosh, where licensed blood centres report their stock.

Your privacy

"Sort by distance" uses your browser's location only inside your browser. Your position is never sent to our server. The public pages set no cookies.

Limitations

  • Details can change between checks. Always call before travelling.
  • Distances are straight-line estimates, not road distances.
  • This site is not an emergency service and does not replace medical advice.
